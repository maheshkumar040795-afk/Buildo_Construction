Who We Are

Our Story

Buildo Interior & Construction was founded with a simple promise — to build beautiful, functional spaces that stand the test of time. From modular kitchens to full residential construction, we bring together design sensibility and engineering discipline under one roof.

Founded by Arwin S and co-founded by Hariharan M, Buildo brings together a shared vision for thoughtful design and dependable execution — built on craftsmanship, integrity and a commitment to every client's dream space.

Our Vision

To be Tamil Nadu's most trusted name in interior design and construction, known for craftsmanship and integrity.

Our Mission

Deliver premium spaces on time and on budget, with transparent pricing and dependable execution.

Quality Commitment

Only trusted, verified material brands go into every Buildo project — no compromises.

Transparent Pricing

Clear per sq.ft packages with itemised inclusions — no hidden surprises.
